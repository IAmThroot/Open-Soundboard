Open Soundboard — portable notes
================================

Everything needed to run is in this folder: just double-click
open-soundboard.exe. Nothing is installed, nothing is written to Program
Files, and there is no uninstaller — delete the folder to remove the app.

Where your stuff lives
----------------------
Your boards, sounds and microphone recordings are NOT inside this folder.
They are stored per-user in:

    %APPDATA%\OpenSoundboard\Open Soundboard\
        config\config.json (boards, hotkeys, volume, device choices)
        data\recordings\   (microphone takes)

Because that folder is separate from the app, you can move this portable
folder anywhere, keep several copies, or upgrade by replacing the .exe — your
sounds always stay put. Deleting this folder does not delete your soundboard.

Notes
-----
* Hotkeys work while the window is minimized or closed to the system tray.
  Turn on tray behavior with the ⚙ (Settings) button in the toolbar.
* Windows may show a SmartScreen warning the first time you run an app that
  was not installed from the Store. Choose "More info" → "Run anyway".
